What We Found on the Way



1. “Prince Charming who grew in one day as much as others in ten!”


A clear Mind in a Strong body.

As Body, Mind and Soul begin to work together again,
the "Prince Charming" effect can appear.

The mind grows calmer and wiser, the body stronger and more balanced,
and the soul more at peace.

This Alchemy can make your work, progress, and talent grow as in the tale – in what you do.



2. We No Longer Miss Childhood.


Oh, how we longed for Childhood!

We used to speak with other adults in melancholy about a bygone era of purity, freedom, and love.


Until... one day, the feeling of missing it was gone. Completely.

Because, in fact, childhood never ended.

It was only that Suffering had appeared. And Concealment.

Once those two begin to dissolve, sweet Childhood can return.
